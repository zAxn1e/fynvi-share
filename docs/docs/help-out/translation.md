# Translating

You can help to translate Fynvi Share into your language.
On [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/fynvi-share) you can easily translate Fynvi Share online.

Is your language not on Crowdin? Feel free to [Request it](https://github.com/zAxn1e/fynvi-share/issues/new?assignees=&labels=language-request&projects=&template=language-request.yml&title=%F0%9F%8C%90+Language+request%3A+%3Clanguage+name+in+english%3E).
