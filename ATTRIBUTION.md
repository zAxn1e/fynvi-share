## Modifications in Fynvi Share

Fynvi Share introduces substantial extensions and redesigns on top of Pingvin Share X 1.22.1, including:
- An atmospheric, accessible **Obsidian Aurora** design system and redesigned user interface.
- Resumable chunked upload architecture with real-time transfer tracking.
- Folder hierarchy management and drag-and-drop folder uploads.
- Human-friendly custom share slugs.
- Enhanced API key management and ShareX custom uploader integration.
- Dedicated migration and database reconciliation tooling.

We extend our deep gratitude to Elias Schneider and all upstream contributors whose foundational open-source work made this project possible.
